product, not a second sale. Metric: revenue per recipient over 14 days and unsubscribe rate (guardrail).

**What changed and why**

- Angle: from "habit and supplement" to "beauty ritual and texture". In France, 29.1 % of women aged 25-54 bought
  beauty products online in the last 3 months of 2024, but only 11.8 % bought medicines or supplements
  (Eurostat isoc_ec_ibgs): the routine is framed next to a serum or a cream, not next to a pill.
- Tone: shorter, warmer, "vous", fewer numbered steps; the brand voice stays the same (plain, no promises).
- The claim: the official French wording of the same authorised claim, word for word.
- In the SIMULATED test (FR, March 2025 - February 2026), the rewritten FR welcome earned more revenue per
  recipient than the version translated from the German flow and had fewer unsubscribes (see the report, page 5).
