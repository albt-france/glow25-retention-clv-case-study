**Two tests**

1. Replenishment day 26 + branch vs day 21 (A/B, Feb-May triggers): 60-day repurchase 56.1 % vs 51.5 %
   (+4.6 points, +/- 1.3). **Decision: rolled out to all markets.**
2. -30 % comeback offer to "too expensive" cancellers vs holdout: return 18.0 % vs 12.7 %, but CM per recipient
   over 6 months EUR 8.14 vs EUR 10.09. **Decision: offer stopped; "upgrade pack" test briefed for November.**

**One decision**

The win-back flow is **not** scaled: January looked strong, but its own holdout rose just as much
(3.7 % vs 4.1 % 30-day reorder). Re-test in March, outside the January peak.

**One learning shared with Acquisition**

Customers whose first order is a subscription are worth about EUR 149 of contribution margin in 12 months, a
one-off supplement buyer about EUR 55 and a trial-pack buyer about EUR 50; influencer-acquired cohorts sit about
7 % below average (EUR 58 vs EUR 62). Acquisition bids should use 12-month CM-CLV by first product, not first-order revenue.
